**Subject:** The app question nobody could answer for me

Hi [FIRST NAME],

[BACKSTORY] You know how much of [THEIR BUSINESS] still runs on paper, spreadsheets and memory. I was the same.

[DESIRE] I wanted one simple app my customers could use: bookings, records, reminders, with my name on it.

[WALL] Every quote was for a hand-made build. Nobody could prove that one customer would never see another customer's records. And nobody could tell me what happens when the licence rules change.

[EPIPHANY] Then I found a factory that builds apps the way car makers build cars: proven parts, the same nine stages every time, and a check at every gate before anything moves on. The walls between businesses and customers are tested after every paid step, from outside the app.

[PLAN] The first step is a short idea form: your vision, who pays, the pain you solve, and twenty plain questions. The factory gives an honest verdict, including "don't build this" when that's the right answer.

[CONFLICT] I nearly didn't send mine. I thought it was too small, or too regulated. That turned out to be exactly what the factory checks for, and why the gates exist.

[ACHIEVEMENT] I got a clear verdict and a plan, before spending anything on building.

[TRANSFORMATION] Now I think about the app as part of the business, not a risky side project.

If you'd like to see whether your idea fits, here's the form: [PARTNER LINK: TO CONFIRM]

Full disclosure: I'm a partner, and I may earn a referral fee if you go ahead. It never changes your price, and nobody is promised any income.

[YOUR NAME]
