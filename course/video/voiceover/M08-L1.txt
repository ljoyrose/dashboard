Sales Page, Domain And Funnels
(Read at about 130 words a minute. UK English.)

Theo is seventeen. He was helping to put together a page for Northvale Canine Society, the club that resells Litterline to its members under its own name. He wanted more club members to try it, so he planned a big button that said free plan. It seemed generous and harmless. But the free plan was refused. Northvale is a reseller, and the factory has a firm rule: a reseller's brand is never on a free plan. That made Theo look much harder at how a button on a page turns into a real, recorded choice.

This is Stage 7, called Launch. It happens in the room called Runway, which is tagged Brand, because this is where the app meets the outside world under each brand's name. The jobs include a sales page, set-up for each brand, the domain, the funnels, and a careful set of launch tests. This lesson covers the outside pieces: the page people land on, the web address they type, and the path that leads them from a first look to signing up. The next lesson covers the launch tests and the Launch gate.

The sales page explains what the app does, who it is for and what it costs. The template line asks for three things: the sales page, a rival line and a launch price. A rival line is a short, honest statement of how this app differs from what people use now. The factory drafts the page, and the factory owner approves it. Much of the wording comes from the research in Stage 1, where customers' own words about their problem were collected. People trust words that sound like their own experience.

A domain is the name of a website, the words people type to find it. Computers find each other by number, so the internet keeps an address book called DNS, the Domain Name System. DNS records point the name at the server where the app lives. The factory picks the domain name in Stage 3, then buys it and sets the records in Stage 4: one for the bare name and one for the www version. SSL, the padlock in the browser that keeps traffic private, is set up too. In Stage 7, all three are verified from outside.

A funnel is a short series of pages that leads a visitor from a first look to signing up or paying. It is wide at the top, where many people arrive, and narrow at the bottom, where fewer people act. The factory builds its landing page and funnel in ClickFunnels, a tool for making sales pages and funnels. The offer was first drafted back in Stage 1 in a funnel shape: who the customer is, the hook, which is their problem in their own words, and the offer itself. That same page is used again here.

How does ClickFunnels talk to the app? The simplest route is a built-in connection that sends sign-ups and tags across. A tag is a label on a contact, such as trial started. One security rule matters a lot: never unlock paid content using a tag that public code can apply, because any visitor could apply it. Another rule: never store a ClickFunnels key or password in the app's database. The app stores only which brand links to which workspace. Paid access is decided by the app itself, never by a label.

Now the plan. Each button on a landing page carries both the brand and the plan inside its link. The plan travels through sign-in the same way the brand does. The rule is sign in first, pay second. When the person comes back, the server checks the plan really is one of that brand's offers, records the chosen plan against the member even if they abandon checkout, and opens that plan's checkout. The price is never taken from the web address, because anyone can edit a web address. Each payment is stamped with the buyer's brand.

Free is decided per brand offer, never for the whole app. The factory owner's own brands may offer a free plan. A reseller's brand never has a free offer. So when Theo's button asked for a free plan on Northvale's brand, the server refused it, exactly as designed. Northvale's members go from sign-in straight to the pay page, then on to their dashboard. To sum up: a sales page the owner approves, a domain proved from outside, funnels in ClickFunnels, and a plan that travels safely with every click.
