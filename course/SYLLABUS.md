## Track F · Family Learners · 11 Modules · 24 Lessons · about 2.4 Hours

| Module | Lesson | Outcome | Min |
|---|---|---|---|
| M00 · What Is An App Factory | M00-L1 · What An App Is Made Of | You can name the four things every app is made of and explain why a factory reuses tested parts. | 6 |
|  | M00-L2 · The Road, Rooms And Gates | You can name the 9 stages, describe the 11 rooms and say which gates need a person to decide. | 6 |
| M01 · Stage 0 · Idea | M01-L1 · The Idea Form | You can write an idea as a one-line vision, a named payer and a clear pain, and name the four doors ideas come through. | 6 |
|  | M01-L2 · The 20 Codes | You can name the 4 code groups, read a code as a word plus a number and say which 6 codes are required. | 6 |
|  | M01-L3 · Kill Check, Verdict And Data Role | You can apply the three kill questions, work out the suggested verdict, name the four data roles and explain the Screen gate. | 6 |
| M02 · Stage 1 · Research | M02-L1 · The Research Sweep | You can list the parts of the research sweep and explain why customers' own words come before our own ideas. | 6 |
|  | M02-L2 · The Merit Gate | You can explain the four Merit verdicts as a decision tree, say why a person decides and describe the DPIA hold. | 6 |
| M03 · Stage 2 · Base | M03-L1 · Picking A Base | You can explain how the factory picks a base, copies the template and passes the Base gate. | 6 |
|  | M03-L2 · Bricks, Shelves And Kits | You can explain what bricks, shelves and kits are, and why K00 sits under every app. | 6 |
| M04 · Stage 3 · Features | M04-L1 · Choosing Kits And Bricks | You can describe how the first version of an app is scoped, mapped and mocked up before Lock. | 6 |
|  | M04-L2 · Duties And The Lock Gate | You can explain the obligations ledger and what must be true before the Lock gate freezes the feature list. | 6 |
| M05 · Stage 4 · Schema | M05-L1 · How The System Makes The Tables | You can explain how codes and kits decide the tables, and how each table is built, set and recorded. | 6 |
|  | M05-L2 · Walls And Table Tests | You can name the eight walls and describe the table tests that must pass before the Schema gate. | 6 |
| M06 · Stage 5 · Data | M06-L1 · Test Identities And Test Tenants | You can explain why every app needs two contrasting test brands and named test members before any paid build starts. | 6 |
|  | M06-L2 · The Leak And Tenant Tests | You can describe a test plan with negative cases, read a test matrix and explain what the Invest gate decides. | 6 |
| M07 · Stage 6 · Prompts | M07-L1 · How Build Prompts Are Written | You can list what every paid build prompt must carry and explain why each prompt does only one job. | 6 |
|  | M07-L2 · After Every Paid Turn | You can put the six after-turn steps in order and explain what the Send gate needs before an app moves on. | 6 |
| M08 · Stage 7 · Launch | M08-L1 · Sales Page, Domain And Funnels | You can explain what a domain, DNS and a funnel are, and why a reseller's brand is never on a free plan. | 6 |
|  | M08-L2 · The Launch Walk And Boosts | You can name the checks that come before the Launch gate and explain why the weekly boost is still only proposed. | 6 |
| M09 · Stage 8 · Operate | M09-L1 · Watching A Live App Daily | You can explain the daily, weekly and monthly checks that watch a live app, and when each one runs. | 6 |
|  | M09-L2 · The Money Line | You can read a monthly money line and explain when the Return rule and the Retire question come in. | 6 |
|  | M09-L3 · Rescue, Retire And Recycle | You can follow a live app through Rescue, Back to Operate, Retire, Recycle, Recovery and Re-imagine. | 7 |
| M10 · Your First Idea | M10-L1 · Filling In Your First Idea Form | You can fill in an idea form, using the fictional Litterline as a worked example. | 7 |
|  | M10-L2 · Where To Go Next | You can describe the Vision 2029 picture and the honest next steps on the value ladder. | 6 |

Every lesson: outcome, Epiphany Bridge opener (light), 7-9 one-idea beats, recap card with Why It Matters, 4-question quiz (quizzes.json), storyboard and voice-over text.
