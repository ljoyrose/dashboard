Privacy policy

RR Marketplace · last updated 25 October 2026

Who runs it

RR Marketplace is a private tool run by Lisa (puppyclassroom.com) to manage her own selling accounts. It is not offered to the public. Contact: lisa@puppyclassroom.com.

What it connects to

With the account holder's permission, given through each marketplace's own sign-in page, it uses the official eBay and Etsy APIs on that account only, to:

  • read the account's own listings, quantities and orders;
  • end, relist or change the quantity of the account's own listings when an item sells elsewhere or comes back into stock;
  • mark an order as posted and add its tracking number.

It never buys anything, never contacts buyers, and never acts on any account other than the one that gave permission.

Buyer information

It does not store buyer names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers or message text. It keeps only order numbers, item numbers and quantities, so the same sale is never handled twice.

Where data is kept

In the account holder's own Google Workspace account (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets). Nothing is sent to any other service. The data is never sold, rented, shared or used for advertising, and there is no tracking.

Access keys

Marketplace access keys are kept in the Apps Script project's private settings. Each key is only ever sent to its own marketplace (eBay keys to api.ebay.com, Etsy keys to openapi.etsy.com).

How long it is kept

Order and item numbers used to avoid repeats are deleted after 90 days. Listing records are the seller's own business records and stay in their Google account until they delete them.

Your control

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the marketplace account's settings (third-party app permissions). To ask a question or to have data deleted, email lisa@puppyclassroom.com.
